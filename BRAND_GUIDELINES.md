# Praise & Peace | Visual Brand Standards

**Status:** User-approved visual direction, based on the November 19, 2026 square devotional design with a sunrise, open Bible, navy-and-gold title treatment, and dove/open-book wordmark.

**Applies to:** All future Praise & Peace Facebook and Instagram feed images, social post templates, and the content approval/publishing workflow.

## 1. Visual identity
- **Display wordmark:** `Praise and Peace` (the project may also be referred to as `Praise & Peace` in instructions).
- **Brand motif:** peaceful dove rising over an open Bible/book, in navy and gold, accompanied by the wordmark. Use the same approved logo asset once supplied. **Do not invent a new logo variation each day.** The approved sample is a visual reference, not a pixel-perfect source logo.
- **Personality:** reverent, warm, hopeful, Scripture-centered, peaceful, premium but not flashy.
- **Visual atmosphere:** believable golden morning light, softly layered hills, pale sky, open Bible on a wooden table, optional discreet foliage/ceramic mug. Rotate scenes to avoid identical compositions while retaining one recognizable brand system.

Avoid neon colors, saturated gradients, crowded decorative effects, and low-contrast text.

## 3. Typography and layout
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 square. Target export 1080×1080 pixels for Facebook and Instagram feeds.
- **Brand header:** Place the same navy/gold dove-book logo and `Praise and Peace` wordmark near the upper center. Keep a generous safe margin, ideally at least 7% of image width.
- **Main text:** Large, high-contrast dark-navy editorial serif; visually similar to a classic book or church-publication serif. Keep fully legible on mobile. Do not assume the sample's exact font family is licensed or available.
- **Scripture reference:** Centered beneath the main message, separated subtly by a fine gold divider or small plant motif.
- **Encouragement:** Short but meaningful Tagalog application text in warm-cream serif within a deep-blue, softly textured brushstroke/banner near the bottom, without covering important parts of the Bible.
- **Image composition:** Open Bible in the lower foreground, golden natural light, quiet scenery. Leave calm negative space for readable typography.
- **Text load:** Prioritize one main message, one reference line, and one short encouraging application. Avoid extra subtitles, lengthy prayers, English Bible text, and hashtags on the image.

## 4. Scripture integrity and image copy
- **Primary Tagalog Bible source:** `Ang Biblia, 2001` (`ABTAG2001`), Philippine Bible Society.
- **Do not invent or truncate verse wording.** Use a complete short ABTAG2001 verse ONLY if exact wording, verse reference, and quotation/copyright permission have been checked against a trusted source. Otherwise use a faithful, **unquoted paraphrase** of the complete thought.
- For a paraphrase, mark the source as `Batay sa [Book Chapter:Verse]`; do **not** format paraphrase as a direct quotation or imply the wording is verbatim ABTAG2001. In the companion devotional/caption, identify it as a summary/paraphrase.
- The user prefers **not** to display the headings `Buod/Paliwanag` or `Hindi direktang sipi` in the image itself. Do not hide what the text is: `Batay sa` source labeling should make its paraphrase status clear without those large headings.
- **Image language:** Tagalog only (except proper names, reference/version abbreviations, and the approved brand wordmark). Never include English KJV verses in the image.
- **Devotional/caption:** Keep Scripture, interpretation, application, and personal encouragement distinguishable. Provide reasonable immediate literary and historical context where appropriate. Never suggest an Israel-specific promise guarantees modern material success. Include the passage reference and a source link.
- Never automatically post an image or caption that contains unverified quoted Bible text or visible spelling errors.

## 5. Reusable image generation brief
"Create a 1080×1080 square Praise and Peace Christian devotional image in the established brand system: consistent top-centered navy-and-antique-gold dove-over-open-book wordmark (use the approved logo asset or reference), elegant legible deep-navy editorial-serif Tagalog main message, fine gold divider, centered Bible reference, rich muted blue brushstroke containing a concise Tagalog encouragement in warm cream, open Bible on a wooden table along the bottom, realistic gentle sunrise and misty hills, subtly framed foliage, quiet reverent atmosphere, natural lighting, generous safe areas, professional mobile readability. Follow the supplied image text verbatim; do not add, remove, invent, or change Bible words. Avoid English quotations and decorative clutter."

## 6. Publishing and approvals
- Create one coordinated image and Tagalog caption suitable for both Facebook and Instagram.
- Content remains `DRAFT` until explicitly approved by the user for its unique post ID (for example, `APPROVED PP-2026-11-19`).
- Only the **specific approved image and caption version** may be published; revisions reset approval. No automatic publishing from content generation alone.
- Before publishing, validate source and scripture claims, visible copy, image dimensions/format, asset public URL, the approved revision, and the target accounts.
- Maintain independent Facebook and Instagram publishing statuses to prevent duplicate posts on retries.
- Do not modify the existing 6:00 AM Manila content schedule or Meta access permissions as a side effect of changing brand standards.

## 7. Pre-publish visual QA
- [ ] Approved brand logo/wordmark is used consistently, without new arbitrary variants
- [ ] Navy, gold, cream and muted blue remain consistent
- [ ] 1:1, mobile readable, safe margins, no clipping or typos
- [ ] Main message is theologically faithful; no incomplete or fabricated verse
- [ ] Correct reference and verse type (exact quote or paraphrase with `Batay sa`)
- [ ] No English verse, excess text, or misleading promises
- [ ] Approved caption/image version and post ID match
- [ ] Manual approval is recorded before either platform publishes

**Asset limitation:** The most recent design is an image-generation reference, not an independently provided official vector/logo file. To make the logo identical in every post, store one explicit approved logo PNG/SVG in the repository and overlay it at a fixed size and position during rendering.
